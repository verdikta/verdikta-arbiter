# A recovery spell shorter than the timelock takes over before the owners can answer

A comment in the spell's constructor says that its delay must be shorter than the timelock's.

The owners' only way to get rid of a spell they did not make is a timelocked proposal, and that takes the whole timelock delay.

With a spell at ten days and a timelock at twenty, the spell runs at day ten while the removal is still waiting. The owners are out.
