# A stake placed in front of a reward payment takes its share

A staker who sees a reward payment coming can stake just ahead of it, collect a share as if it had been there all along, and exit early.

The early-exit penalty takes part of the stake. When the reward is worth more, that is a profit for locking nothing.

The test has a long-term staker with 1,000 tokens, an attacker who stakes as much just before 1,000 reward tokens arrive, and reads what the attacker collects.
